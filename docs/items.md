# The verified list

Twenty items. What has to be done is the same for anyone moving in Israel; only
the route to the local authority changes, and only for four of them.

Most of this comes from the כל-זכות guide to moving house, which is maintained
with the support of the Ministry of Justice and the National Digital Agency and
was last updated in July 2026. Where an item does not come from there, it says so.

Nothing is added to this file by an agent. If something is missing, I add it.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/מדריך_למעבר_דירה

## Order

Two items must come before others, and this is not a dependency the system
enforces — it is guidance shown on the item itself.

1. The population registry comes first. National Insurance will not accept a new
   address before the Interior Ministry has it.
2. Arnona before water.

## The items

### 1 · Population registry — change of address

Authority: Population and Immigration Authority.
Routes: online through the government identification system, self-service
stations, a bureau, or by post. The updated ID appendix arrives within five
working days.

Warning to show on the item: post sent to the registered address counts as
delivered. A fine that arrived at the old address cannot be disputed afterwards.

### 2 · National Insurance

Only after item 1 is done. Personal area online, or form T900 by post or at a
branch.

### 3 · Arnona — change of occupier

**Route depends on `Sug_Muni`.**

Needs: a rental contract of twelve months or more, signed by both sides; copies
of both identity documents; an arnona voucher showing the property number.

Warnings to show on the item:
- The twelve months are counted from the date of notice. A contract cannot be
  submitted retroactively.
- If this is not done, the account stays in the previous occupier's name and
  accrues debt.
- It must be reported even when moving within the same authority.

### 4 · Parking permit

**Route depends on `Sug_Muni`.** Available once registered with the authority.

### 5 · Arnona discount

**Route depends on `Sug_Muni`.** Only if eligible.

### 6 · Registration for kindergarten or school

**Route depends on `Sug_Muni`.** Only if relevant.

### 7 · Certificate of residence

Only in periphery settlements, for the income tax credit. Granted after twelve
months of residence, backdated to the start.

### 8 · Electricity

Israel Electric Corporation. Routes: the website, a form by email or fax to
1-800-200-103, or by telephone to 103.

Bring: the contract or account number from the top of the bill, a current meter
reading, and personal details.

### 9 · Water

The municipal water corporation. Comes after arnona.

### 10 · Gas

The company that supplies the new apartment.

### 11 · Communications

Telephone, infrastructure, internet provider, television.

## Routes by authority type

Applies to items 3, 4, 5 and 6 only. The rest are identical everywhere.

**City (עירייה)** — an online form on the municipal site, or the 106 call centre.

**Local council (מועצה מקומית)** — usually by telephone to the council offices.

**Regional council (מועצה אזורית)** — the council, and the local committee of the
settlement as well. Two bodies, not one.

These three descriptions are my own and have not been checked against a real
authority. Verify against Tel Aviv before shipping.
